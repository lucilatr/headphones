How the World Works

Two worlds, one wheel

The World of Music is a vast realm built entirely by music, for music. Our world and theirs feed each other endlessly: "Our world inspires the music created in your world… which, in turn, adds something to ours. And on and on it goes — 'round and 'round, like a wheel."

Every song matters. "A number one hit raises a mountain. A catchy tune builds a bridge. Even someone singing in the shower can patch a pothole or fix a broken traffic light." The world is organized by genre — hundreds of them — each rendered as its own city, region, or natural wonder.

The way in — the headphones

Zoe crosses over through her late father Miles's favorite can headphones. She puts them on, presses the power button, the music plays — and the attic warps and spirals into a portal (The Vortex) that carries her into the World of Music. She can rip the headphones off at any moment to snap back to the real world.

The Five Pieces of a Song
Miles left his song unfinished. To protect it, Zoe must gather the five elements every song is made of.
The Antagonist — The Silence

The Silence

Not a villain — a force. Songs that aren't loved and shared fade away, and The Silence slips in with "creepy dark fingers of nothingness" to snuff them out. Unfinished songs are the most fragile of all — they unravel into pieces, which the Silence hunts down one by one.

It manifests as Tendrils of Silence: roiling fingers of silent static — lightless, soundless, edges shivering with distortion — that suck sound "like little mini black holes." At full force it becomes a storm of static and black lightning that lays siege to the Conservatory of Sound.

A World Built in Layers
The music history you can literally walk through — older eras melt into newer ones.
The Old Masters
Beethoven, Mozart, griots, sitar / guqin / shamisen players. They laid the foundations.
The Geniuses
Presley, Franklin, Hendrix, Lennon, Shakur, Marley, Piaf, Cruz. They built the cities and gave the genres their shape.
The Icons
Beyoncé, Swift, Lamar, Bad Bunny and more. The genres expand, collide and fuse into hybrids.
